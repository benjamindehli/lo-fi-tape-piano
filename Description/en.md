Lo-fi Tape Piano is a Louis Zwicki Pianette recorded to a cassette tape machine with its preamps pushed slightly into overdrive. The tape adds compression, harmonics and pitch variation, so what comes back is closer to a Mellotron or a Chamberlin than to a well-regulated grand piano. If you want a perfectly voiced piano, this is not it.

Three microphones ran at once, and each is a separate layer you can mix: a telephone microphone and a gooseneck microphone in mono, and a condenser pair in stereo. Blend them for anything from thin and boxy to a fuller, roomier picture of the instrument.

246 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit plus two impulse responses, stored as lossless FLAC in one sample file that the installer puts in place. This is the oldest library in the range and it began life as an EXS24 instrument in 2020, became a Decent Sampler library in 2025, and is now a plugin.

## Features

- Louis Zwicki Pianette recorded to cassette tape through slightly overdriven preamps.
- Tape compression, harmonics, wow and flutter baked into the samples.
- Three microphone layers with independent levels: telephone mic and gooseneck mic in mono, condensers in stereo.
- Impulse-response effects.
- Mellotron and Chamberlin character rather than a clean piano.
- 246 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored losslessly (639 MB source library).
